## Open: allprint

`allprint` (both libraries) comes from `oldcc/allprint/0_1.s` and `oldcc/allprint.s/0_1.s`, cas sources cut from the original
members (`sdis.py -a`; references to odd data offsets written as label + distance; segmented: the cutter of the slibc work,
jump table entries as `.long`). What is known:

- `putc` in the default case needs a `stdio.h` that WEGA does not ship: the form of the shipped header
  (`--cnt < 0 ? _flsbuf(...) : (*_ptr++ = x)`, `src/head/stdio.h`) with `char *_ptr` (the result is sign-extended, `extsb`).
  The old copies (`src/cmd/cparse/stdio.h` and others) have `char *_ptr` but the `>= 0 ? ... : _flsbuf` form; the shipped one
  has the `< 0` form with `unsigned char *_ptr`. A header of the time between them (a guess) gives the right `putc`.
- Left with that header and ccv1: popt places the blocks of the switch otherwise. The original keeps the `fprintf`
  arguments of the default case behind the `printable` test (`jr ne` to the `putc`) and puts the fast path of `putc` before
  its `_flsbuf` call; ccv1 moves the `fprintf` block next to the shared `call _fprintf`. Tried without success: all 120 orders
  of the cases, the `if` inverted, `break`/`return` forms, `== 0`; ccv1q, ccv1r, ccv0, ccv0p, cc (with both headers).
- Next: `host/recon/poptrace.sh` (how popt treats the function; `HOWTO_reconstruct.md`, phase B) on allprint with v1; the
  extern order (`_yyout, _fprintf, __flsbuf`) says the `fprintf` of the default case comes before the `putc` in the source,
  as in System III.
