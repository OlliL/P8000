# cas: findings

Generated by `host/recon/findings.py cas` from `findings.json`, do not edit.

Against /bin/cas, built with sccvc -O (parser.y and assemble.c without -O), the working compiler of cas (OLDCC.md), commit 1c633edb. 155 functions: 52 same, 13 close, 90 open.

/bin/cas was built by an older scc than the shipped one, which we do not have as such; sccvc is the nearest found (src/TODO.md, step 10): byte identity is not reached. The behaviour is the same: the corpus (`host/tools/corpus.sh cas`) and the tests of tools/test. The differences that are not differences of the source are the classes D1 ... D13 of `host/OLDCC.md`.

The compiler (measured 2026-11-09, the model in `progcfg.py`, `strictcmp.py` over the 155 functions): the shipped scc gives 2 functions strictly equal, 16 masked, 9228 differing lines; `sccvc` 24, 47 and 5412, with the text size of the original. Its switches are those of the old compilers v0/v0p (`OLDCC.md`, section 2): /bin/cas was probably built by a segmented compiler of that age (a guess). The notes below are measured with sccvc. Still counted as differences: the data addresses of the model. The own modules have the data size of the original; the model links the shipped slibc, of whose 57 functions behind the own code 28 are found unchanged in /bin/cas and 29 not (`doprnt`, `malloc`, `filbuf`, `flsbuf`, `fread` and others): /bin/cas holds an older slibc, as /bin/sld does. The 46 bytes of data in excess of the original, against 46 bytes less bss, are in this part (the SCCS string of the shipped `malloc`).

same: equal in the normal form of `host/tools/cmpmod.py` (addresses, labels, frame sizes, scratch registers masked). close: the same calls, constants and variables. open: lines = differing instructions. Instr: ours/original.
