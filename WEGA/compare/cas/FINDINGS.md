# cas: findings

Generated by `host/recon/findings.py cas` from `findings.json`, do not edit.

Against /bin/cas, built with sccvc -O (parser.y and assemble.c without -O), the working compiler of cas (OLDCC.md), commit d6569287. 155 functions: 83 same, 6 close, 66 open.

/bin/cas was built by an older scc than the shipped one, which we do not have as such; sccvc is the nearest found (src/TODO.md, step 10): byte identity is not reached. The behaviour is the same: the corpus (`host/tools/corpus.sh cas`) and the tests of tools/test. The differences that are not differences of the source are the classes D1 ... D13 of `host/OLDCC.md`.

The compiler (measured 2026-11-09, the model in `progcfg.py`, `strictcmp.py` over the 155 functions): the shipped scc gives 2 functions strictly equal, 16 masked, 9228 differing lines; `sccvc` with `slibc-v11` 89 strictly equal, 90 masked, 1759 lines (2026-11-09), with the text, data and bss sizes of the original. Data: 36374 of 38360 bytes equal at the same address; the rest are code addresses in jump and pointer tables (they follow the code). Bit 31 of the addresses in `.addr` (also the jump tables, which the spopt of sccvc writes as `.addr`) comes from `OLD_D13`; the value lists of a cpir switch right in front of its targets from `OLD_D6T`; the target of a dense jump table in the pair of the value from `OLD_D6J` (`OLDCC.md`). Its switches are those of the old compilers v0/v0p (`OLDCC.md`, section 2): /bin/cas was probably built by a segmented compiler of that age (a guess). The notes below are measured with sccvc. The data and bss sizes are those of the original since `slibc-v11` (the members that differ from the shipped slibc are cut from /bin/cas: `src/lib/oldslibc/*/0_2.s`, `host/tools/oldslibc_cas.py`).

same: equal in the normal form of `host/tools/cmpmod.py` (addresses, labels, frame sizes, scratch registers masked). close: the same calls, constants and variables. open: lines = differing instructions. Instr: ours/original.
